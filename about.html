Step 02

About User Testing

This testing is designed to evaluate Smart MUET Guide V2 as a digital MUET exam-preparation guide. We are interested in how easy the guide is to use, how clear the information is, how useful the exam tips and strategies are, and how well the guide supports your MUET preparation.

Why your participation matters

Useful feedback

Your feedback helps identify what works well and what needs improvement.

Real user experience

Your experience as a MUET candidate helps us evaluate the guide in a realistic context.

Better next version

The findings will guide evidence-based improvements to Smart MUET Guide V2.

Confidentiality

Responses are intended to be analysed collectively, without identifying individual participants.

Important

  • We are testing the guide, not your ability.
  • There are no right or wrong answers.
  • Please be honest about anything that is confusing or difficult to use.
  • You may stop participating at any time.
