Smart MUET Guide V2 — User Testing Study

🎯 Study Purpose

This study evaluates the Smart MUET Guide V2, a digital learning tool designed to support Malaysian matriculation students in preparing for the MUET (Malaysian University English Test).

Your feedback will help us improve the app's usability, clarity, and effectiveness in supporting your MUET preparation.

📋 What Participation Involves

  • Baseline Survey (5 min) — Your background and familiarity with MUET
  • Explore the App (45–60 min) — Work through practice tasks for Speaking, Reading, Listening, and Writing
  • Feedback Questionnaire (10–15 min) — Share your experience and suggestions

Total estimated time: 60–90 minutes

⚖️ Your Rights as a Participant

  • Voluntary Participation: You may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty or explanation.
  • Right to Refuse: You can choose not to answer any question.
  • No Deception: This study contains no hidden procedures or deception.
  • No Coercion: Your participation will not affect your grades or academic standing.

🔒 Confidentiality & Data Protection

Your privacy is our highest priority:

  • No Personal Identifiers: Your responses will be anonymous. We will not collect your name, student ID, or personal email.
  • Data Storage: Your responses are stored securely in Google Forms and linked to your device (localStorage) only during this session.
  • Data Retention: Responses will be retained for 6 months for analysis, then permanently deleted.
  • Authorized Access: Only the research team at POLIPD has access to aggregated, anonymized results.
  • No Sharing: Your individual responses will not be shared with third parties.

✨ Benefits & Risks

Benefits:

  • Early access to an innovative MUET preparation tool
  • Personalized feedback on your speaking, reading, listening, and writing
  • Your input will shape a tool designed to support students like you

Risks: Minimal. You may experience mild discomfort if you find certain test components challenging, but there are no physical or psychological risks.
