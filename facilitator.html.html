👨‍🏫 Facilitator Guide

Running the Smart MUET Guide V2 User Testing Session

📚 What Is This Testing Session?

This is a usability and feedback study where matriculation students test the Smart MUET Guide V2 digital learning app and provide structured feedback on its effectiveness.

Your role as facilitator: Manage the session flow, answer questions, ensure ethical compliance, and collect data.

Duration: 90 minutes per session (groups of 5–15 students recommended)

📋 Pre-Test Setup (48 Hours Before)

Use this checklist to prepare:

⏱️ Session Timeline (90 Minutes Total)

00:00–10 min
Welcome & Orientation
Greet students. Explain the study purpose, benefits, and that participation is voluntary. Distribute or display the consent form.
10–15 min
Consent Collection
Students read consent and agree digitally (tick checkbox on journey.html). Record consent status. Answer any questions about privacy/withdrawal.
15–20 min
Baseline Survey
Students complete baseline form (programme, semester, MUET familiarity, preparation level). Monitor for completion.
20–75 min
Explore Smart MUET Guide
Students navigate the app independently (55 minutes). They should explore all four components (Speaking, Reading, Listening, Writing). Circulate to observe, answer Q&As, note usability issues.
75–90 min
Feedback Survey & Wrap-Up
Students complete the feedback questionnaire (Google Form). Collect any final observations. Thank participants and dismiss.

🎬 What to Say & Do During Testing

Welcome Script (First 5 minutes):

"Welcome, everyone. Thank you for taking part in this study.
Today you'll test a new app called 'Smart MUET Guide V2' that helps prepare
for the MUET exam. Your honest feedback is very valuable to us.

This is NOT a test of you — it's a test of the app. There are no right or wrong
answers. If something doesn't work or is confusing, that's exactly what we need
to know so we can improve it.

Your participation is completely voluntary. You can stop at any time without
any penalty. All your responses will be kept private and anonymous."

During App Exploration:

  • Observe silently: Watch how students navigate. Note any confusion, clicks that don't work, pauses.
  • Don't guide: If a student gets stuck, ask "What are you trying to do?" rather than directing them.
  • Record issues: Note any errors, broken links, or confusing moments in your facilitator notes.
  • Be available: Position yourself where students can easily ask questions.
  • Encourage exploration: If a student finishes early, say: "Have you tried the Reading section? Or Writing? Any component you're curious about?"

Responding to Questions:

Student: "Is this the right answer?"
You: "The app will show you feedback. What do you think?"

Student: "The button doesn't work."
You: "Good catch — we need to know that. Let's note it down. Try the next section?"

Student: "Can I skip Reading and do Writing instead?"
You: "Absolutely — explore whatever interests you most."

✅ After Testing (Wrap-Up)

  • Collect attendance: Confirm all participants signed in.
  • Verify feedback submissions: Check the Google Form responses (open the form results or shared sheet).
  • Document any verbal feedback: Write down any comments students mentioned.
  • Note technical issues: List any app errors, broken links, or usability problems you observed.
  • Send thank-you message: Thank students for their time and honest feedback.
  • Report to Julie: Send facilitator report within 24 hours.

🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

❌ Portal won't load

  • Check internet connection (WiFi working?)
  • Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Clear browser cache (Ctrl+Shift+Delete)
  • Contact Julie if issue persists

❌ Students can't open the Smart MUET Guide app

  • Confirm the main app URL: https://juliemarlina-collab.github.io/Smart-MUET-Guide/
  • Wait 10–15 seconds for the app to load (GitHub Pages can be slow)
  • Try a different computer/browser
  • Ask students to accept any pop-ups or warnings

❌ Google Form isn't loading in the feedback section

  • The form should appear as an embedded view in the page
  • If it doesn't, students can click "Open in New Tab" link as a fallback
  • Make sure their responses are submitted (check form results after session)

❌ Student forgot their progress or page reloaded

  • Explain that progress is saved in the browser (localStorage)
  • Clearing browser cache will reset progress
  • They can go back and re-complete sections (it's OK)

❌ A student wants to withdraw

  • Thank them for their honesty and willingness to participate
  • Confirm no penalty applies
  • Ask if they want to delete their data from the form (optional)
  • Note withdrawal in your attendance records

🎯 Key Reminders

  • Ethical: Maintain student confidentiality. No names are collected.
  • Supportive: Create a comfortable, low-stress environment. It's not a performance test.
  • Observant: Watch for usability issues. Silent observation is gold data.
  • Responsive: Answer questions about the app, privacy, or withdrawal process promptly.
  • Organized: Keep attendance, timings, and issue notes clear.
  • Responsive to Julie: Send reports and log any concerns within 24 hours.
