ℹ️ About This Testing Session

Smart MUET Guide V2 User Testing Study

🎯 What is Smart MUET Guide V2?

Smart MUET Guide V2 is a digital learning application designed to help Malaysian matriculation and diploma students prepare for the MUET (Malaysian University English Test) exam.

The app covers all four MUET components: Speaking, Reading, Listening, and Writing. Each component includes interactive practice tasks, model answers, self-assessment tools, and feedback mechanisms aligned with the official MUET band descriptors and language functions framework.

This is Version 2 — we've improved the design, added new features, and restructured the content based on user feedback. Today, we're testing it with you to ensure it works well and is truly helpful!

🔍 Why Are We Testing This?

Your honest feedback is essential. By testing the app with real students like you, we can:

  • Identify usability problems (confusing buttons, missing information, broken links)
  • Understand which features are most helpful and which need improvement
  • Ensure the MUET information is clear and accurate
  • Make sure the app actually prepares you for success
  • Shape the future of learning tools for Malaysian students

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this a test of me? Will my score affect my grades?

No! This is not an exam or graded assessment. We're testing the app, not you. Your responses are completely anonymous and will never be shared with your instructors or affect your academic standing.

Q: Do I have to answer every question?

No. You can skip any question or section if you're uncomfortable. You can also withdraw from the study at any time without penalty. Just let your facilitator know.

Q: Will my name or personal information be collected?

No. We only collect your programme, semester, and MUET familiarity level. Your actual name, student ID, and personal email are never collected. All responses are completely anonymous.

Q: What if the app crashes or I get stuck?

Tell your facilitator immediately! Technical issues are exactly what we need to hear about. They'll help you troubleshoot or move to a different computer if needed.

Q: Can I share what I learn with my classmates?

Absolutely! The app is designed to be shared. You can talk about the learning experience, but please keep your specific feedback private (so other testers form their own opinions).

Q: How long will this take?

The entire session is 90 minutes maximum. You'll spend about 55 minutes exploring the app at your own pace. If you finish early, you can leave once you've submitted your feedback.

Q: What if I finish the app early?

Great! You can explore other components you haven't tried yet, review the tips, or relax while others finish. Your facilitator will let you know when to move to the feedback survey.

Q: Will I get feedback on my MUET practice answers?

Yes! The app includes model answers, explanations, and feedback for each component. This is part of your learning experience during the testing session.

Q: What happens with my feedback after I submit it?

Your feedback is stored securely in Google Forms and analyzed by the research team at POLIPD. We'll use it to improve the app before it's released more widely. Individual responses are kept confidential and never shared.

Q: When will I see the improved app?

We'll analyze all feedback over the next few months and release Version 2.1 (improved) later this year. You'll be among the first to know!
